# ♥ Support Good Bila

**Help bring a backyard game to life on mobile.**

Good Bila already has a playable prototype: physics-based marbles, cards that change each shot, obstacles, and rising score targets. The next step is to make those mechanics more balanced, readable, and consistent across devices.

[Meet the game and see screenshots](README.md) · [Follow development](DEVELOPMENT.md)

## Ways to get involved

| Type of support | How it helps |
|---|---|
| Play and give feedback | Understand difficulty, interface clarity, combinations, and issues on real devices. |
| Share the game | Introduce the prototype to people who enjoy strategy, physics, and score-chasing games. |
| Collaborate | Contribute art, animation, audio, programming, accessibility work, testing, or translation. |
| Production or publishing partnership | Discuss polish, distribution, communication, and development milestones. |
| Investment or sponsorship | Discuss resources and goals for the next phase of development. |

## What a next development phase could support

- **More Android testing:** devices, resolutions, performance, and compatibility.
- **Balancing:** level difficulty, effect strength, and score progression.
- **Polish:** art, sound effects, animations, and clearer feedback during play.
- **Content and presentation:** additional card possibilities, communication, and distribution preparation.

These are development priorities rather than fixed delivery commitments. Scope, funding, and any agreed benefits will be discussed directly with the creator.
