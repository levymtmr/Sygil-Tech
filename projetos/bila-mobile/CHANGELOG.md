# Recent changes · Good Bila

## 0.23.0
- Heavy destroys an intact target on direct impact while retaining shorter range.
- Explosive destroys its core, cracks outer targets and scatters survivors.
- Giant has a larger body, more mass and a wider damage sweep.
- Replace Curve with Frost; add Lightning and Repulsor, with matching colors, animations and icons.
- Expand the random collection to 13 cards and 11 effects.

## 0.22.0
- English interface: menus, cards, objectives, gameplay, settings, and game over.
- English showcase, support pages, issue forms, release notes, and screenshots.
- Existing player profiles, settings, and best scores remain compatible.

## 0.21.0
- Collection replaces deck building; three random collection cards are drawn each level.
- Read-only gallery and details for all 11 cards.
- Game over with animated score, shattered marble, run summary, and retry flow.

## 0.20.0
- Destruction becomes the only mode.
- Giant, Piercing, Magnetic, Explosive, and Ricochet effects.
- Matching colors, animations, icons, and behavior for the player marble.

## 0.19.0
- Temporary notice when cards run out.
- Red maximum-power gauge with a local shake and 20% stronger shots.

## 0.18.0
- Player marble body colored by effect, without a floating emblem.
- Horizontal gauge near the start of the trajectory.

## 0.15.0–0.17.0
- Opening marble rain and random placement across the field.
- Opening-card focus, icons, and visual effect refinements.

## 0.14.0
- First Destruction prototype and collapsible hand.

Earlier versions explored triangle-marble gameplay, objectives, and multipliers. Classic mode was later removed.
